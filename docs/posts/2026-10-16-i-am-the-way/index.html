I Am the Way

October 16, 2026 · Morning · 3 min read

Scripture John 14:6

  • discipleship
  • trust
  • gospel
  • invitation

Friday morning often arrives with a quiet map question. The week has nearly run its course. Some roads you meant to take are still unfinished. Some turns you did take left you unsure. You may wake already scanning for the right next step — the wise word, the clean decision, the feeling that you are finally on the right track.

Jesus spoke into that exact uncertainty on the night before the cross. Thomas had just said what many of us feel: Lord, we do not know where You are going; how can we know the way? Jesus did not hand him a diagram. He handed him Himself.

“Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.” (John 14:6, KJV)

Not a path you invent

I am the way. That sentence rearranges Friday. The Christian life is not first a skill you master or a route you draw with perfect foresight. It is a Person you follow. The disciples were anxious about destinations and logistics. Jesus answered with nearness. The Way is not floating somewhere ahead of you, waiting to be discovered by the clever. The Way is standing with you, already enough for this morning.

So if you feel directionless, you are not automatically faithless. You may simply be hungry for what only Jesus can be. You do not have to manufacture certainty before you take the next faithful step. You come to Him. You stay near Him. The path becomes clearer in His company than it ever does in your own planning alone.

Truth you can lean on

He is also the truth. Friday can fill with competing voices — what you should have done, what others expect, what fear predicts. Jesus does not add one more opinion to the noise. He is the solid word beneath it. When your own heart argues with itself, His character does not shift. He remains true when your feelings rise and fall. Walking with Him means letting His truth steady you more than the day’s commentary.

Life that starts before the list

And He is the life. Not life as a vague inspiration, but life as communion with the Father through the Son. The deepest need this morning is not a finished inbox. It is living contact with the One who brings you to God. “No man cometh unto the Father, but by me” is not a cold barrier. It is an open door with a Name on it. Jesus does not point toward the Father from a distance. He brings you.

Beginning Friday with Him

How do you receive a word like this before the day thickens?

Tell Him where you feel lost without pretending otherwise. Then receive His claim as personal: Jesus, You are the Way for me today. I do not see the whole road. I trust You with the next step. Be my truth when other voices get loud. Be my life when I feel thin.

Then walk into ordinary Friday with Him — the commute, the quiet work, the conversation you would rather skip, the small kindness nobody notices. Following the Way rarely looks dramatic at 8:30 in the morning. It looks like staying near Jesus while the map is still incomplete.

You do not have to invent the path. You only have to walk with the One who is the Way — and let Him lead you toward the Father through this Friday.

A Friday blessing

May His presence give you direction where the week feels unfinished. May His truth quiet the voices that pull you every which way. And may His life hold you today, not because you finally figured everything out, but because Jesus is the Way — and He is with you on it.

A prayer

Lord Jesus, as this day begins, draw me near. Meet me in John 14:6, and let Your word rest in my heart. Teach me to walk with You in trust and joy.
