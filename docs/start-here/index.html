Jesus is the Son of God who came close to ordinary people. He healed, forgave, taught with authority and kindness, and gave His life so we could be brought home to God. He is alive. He still invites anyone who is curious, tired, or hurting: Come and see.

You do not need to have everything figured out. A next step can be small.

  3. Today’s reflection — a quiet morning, midday, or evening word for this day.
