He Cannot Take It Back

Source International Mission Board, November 2026 Prayer Points: A boda-boda Bible study is reaching Uganda's drivers for Christ (CC BY-NC 4.0)

Recent decades · Romans 10:9 · November 11, 2026 · 3 min read

In Uganda, the quickest way across town is often a boda-boda, a motorcycle taxi. Hundreds of thousands of them zip through the country’s streets. Most of the drivers are men between 25 and 45.

Issa was one of them: 40 years old, a Muslim, earning his living on a motorbike.

One day another driver invited him to a Bible study at the home of two missionaries, James and Sonya Herron. The other driver told him the missionaries genuinely cared about boda-boda drivers.

Issa had good reason to stay away. Only a month earlier, a man in his town had been attacked and badly injured by his own family for leaving Islam to follow Jesus. Everyone knew what had happened.

He went anyway. He felt he had nothing to lose.

What surprised him first was the welcome. Everyone was kind to him, even the missionaries. In his town, he said, Muslims never welcome or help Christians, and he hadn’t expected Christians to welcome him either. “On the first day,” the Herrons shared, “Issa introduced himself and boldly proclaimed to the group that he wanted to learn about Jesus.”

He kept coming. He became a regular member of what the Herrons call the boda-boda Bible study. They have been reaching out to drivers since 2020, and this series ran for six months. By the end of it, 31 men, many of them Muslims, had professed faith in Jesus Christ. Issa was one of them.

At the final gathering each man received his own Bible. Issa says he reads his regularly.

He is honest that he is afraid. He worries about how his family and other Muslims will respond. He knows about the violence in his town against Christians and against Muslims who convert. Nothing about his situation has become safe.

But he says he cannot take back his confession that Christ is Lord. And he enjoys being part of a group where he is learning about God.

That is a short sentence for a big thing. Paul wrote, “If you will confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord, and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved” (Romans 10:9, WEB). For Issa, those words are not abstract. Saying them out loud cost something, and he said them anyway. Once you have seen who Jesus is, it is hard to unsee Him.

Peter wrote to believers who were suffering for the same confession: “But may the God of all grace, who called you to his eternal glory by Christ Jesus, after you have suffered a little while, perfect, establish, strengthen, and settle you” (1 Peter 5:10, WEB).

Please pray for Issa and others in Uganda who are risking everything to follow Jesus, for wisdom for the Herrons as they lead the next Bible study series, and for many more boda-boda drivers to hear the gospel and trust in Christ. If Jesus has met you somewhere you didn’t expect, you are welcome to share your story.
