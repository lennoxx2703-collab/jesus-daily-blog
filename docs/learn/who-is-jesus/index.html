Who is Jesus?

Lesson 1 · About 8 minutes

Meet Jesus in plain words — Son of God, friend of ordinary people, and the One who still says Come and see.

Before you begin

You do not need a degree, a perfect past, or a quiet mind to learn who Jesus is. Bring curiosity. Bring questions. Bring the day you are actually living. Read slowly. Pause when a sentence feels alive. There is no quiz at the end.

Meeting Him in plain words

Jesus is not a vague idea or a distant hero from a stained-glass window. The Gospels show a real person who walked dusty roads, ate with friends, touched the sick, welcomed children, and spoke to people others ignored. He is the Son of God — fully God and fully human — who came close on purpose.

He did not wait for people to clean themselves up first. Fishermen, tax collectors, the grieving, the ashamed, the proud, and the poor all met Him. Some followed right away. Some argued. Some came at night with quiet questions. He stayed patient with all of them.

When Scripture names Him, it names His heart. He is the Good Shepherd who knows His sheep by name. He is the bread of life. He is the way home to the Father. These are not slogans. They are pictures of how He holds us.

What He did — and why it matters

Jesus taught with authority and kindness. He healed bodies and lifted heavy hearts. He forgave sins. He told the truth about God and about us — that we need mercy, and that mercy is offered. Then He went to the cross. He gave His life so we could be brought home to God. That is not a side detail. It is the center of the story.

And He did not stay in the grave. He rose. He is alive. The Christian faith is not only remembering a good teacher. It is trusting a living Lord who still invites people: Come and see.

The World English Bible records His words this way:

“I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father, except through me.” — John 14:6

That claim is large. You do not have to pretend you understand every part of it on day one. You can take the next honest step: listen to Him, ask Him to show you who He is, and keep coming back.

How people begin

Some people meet Jesus in a sudden turn. Many more meet Him the way you meet a friend — slowly, through shared time. You read a little Scripture. You speak a short prayer. You notice His kindness in a hard week. Faith often grows like that: not by forcing a feeling, but by staying near.

If you are unsure, that is okay. Doubt is not a closed door. Bring it with you. Jesus did not turn away honest seekers. Keep asking. Keep reading a Gospel. Keep saying what is true before God.

You do not need to master every title for Jesus today. Learn His name. Learn His welcome. Learn that He came for people like you.

Try this

Take five quiet minutes. Sit somewhere you will not be rushed. Speak out loud or in your heart:

Jesus, I want to know who You are. Show me Your heart. Help me trust You one step at a time.

Then read John 1:35–51 in a Bible if you have one nearby — or open a Gospel and read one short scene of Jesus meeting someone. Notice what He does first: He looks, He invites, He stays.

Write one sentence: Today I saw that Jesus… Keep it simple. That sentence is enough for today.
