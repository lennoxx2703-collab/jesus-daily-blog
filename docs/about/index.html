About

Walking with Jesus exists so people can meet Jesus in Scripture — not as a distant idea, but as the living Lord who speaks peace, truth, and love. These pages are a quiet place to come and see. If you are just beginning, try Start here (or Willkommen auf Deutsch). Common seeker questions live on the FAQ.

Each day brings three short invitations meant to be read slowly: a morning, midday, and evening reflection. Every article includes a passage of Scripture, a pastoral thought, and a hopeful takeaway you can carry into ordinary life. The tone is warm and reverent, never loud — an invitation to draw near, not a lecture from afar.

If you are hurting, begin with Hard times — short verses for anxiety, grief, fear, and other hard days — or a simple prayer on Pray. Each week we also publish Stories of real heart-change encounters with Jesus — across history and especially recent decades — each with a cited source. You can sit with longer readings of Jesus’ teaching, browse by date or tags, or search the archive. Prefer a feed reader? Subscribe to the RSS feed.

If someone you love is struggling, consider sharing the Hard times page — or a single tag link for what they face. A small invitation can be enough. More gentle share links live on the Share page.

Whether you are curious about Jesus, returning after a long time away, or already walking closely with Him, you are welcome here. Come and see.

“I am the way, and the truth, and the life.” — John 14:6
