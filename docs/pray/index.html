Pray

You do not need special words. These short prayers are here if you want company while you speak with Jesus. Read one slowly. Change a name. Leave a sentence out. He already knows the rest.

  • Thanksgiving

    Lord Jesus, thank You for this day and for gifts I almost walked past. Thank You for breath, for people who love me, and for mercy that met me before I asked. Teach my heart to notice Your kindness and to answer with simple gratitude.

  • A hard day

    Jesus, this day is heavy, and I do not have many words. I bring You the tiredness, the worry, and the part of me that wants to hide. Sit with me. Give me rest that is not earned, and enough light for the next small step.

    “Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest.” — Matthew 11:28 WEB

    Related: exhaustion · anxiety

  • For a friend

    Lord Jesus, I hold before You someone I love. You know their name, their need, and what I cannot fix. Draw near to them with comfort and courage. If they are hurting, let them feel they are not alone — and show me how to be a gentle friend.

    Related: Hard times · loneliness · grief

  • For guidance

    Jesus, I cannot see the whole path, and I do not want to lean only on my own understanding. Make the next step plain, and keep me close if I have to wait. Lead me in Your love, not in my hurry.

    “Trust in Yahweh with all your heart, and don’t lean on your own understanding.” — Proverbs 3:5 WEB

    Related: waiting · doubt

  • Confession

    Lord Jesus, I come without hiding. I have sinned in thought, in word, and in what I left undone. Forgive me. Wash what I cannot clean, and give me a heart that turns toward You again. Thank You that You are faithful and kind.

    “If we confess our sins, he is faithful and righteous to forgive us the sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.” — 1 John 1:9 WEB

    Related: shame

  • Morning

    Jesus, this morning is Yours before it is mine. Walk with me through ordinary hours — in work, in rest, and in the people I will meet. Keep my heart soft and my steps near You.

    “They are new every morning. Great is your faithfulness.” — Lamentations 3:23 WEB

  • Evening

    Stay with us, Lord, for the day is ending. I give You what went well and what I could not finish. Forgive what was unkind, and quiet my mind for sleep. Keep those I love through the night, and let me rest in Your peace.

If a day is especially hard, sit first with Hard times. New here? Start here. Prefer a longer word? Try the readings.

“I am the way, and the truth, and the life.” — John 14:6
